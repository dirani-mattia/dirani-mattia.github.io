About me

Hello everyone! I'm Mattia, a mathematics graduate from Italy, currently living in Munich. My main interests are quantitative finance, especially option pricing and optimal portfolio allocation, and AI safety. I'm currently deepening my knowledge of AI alignment and ethics. I hope to model how AI systems respond to morally ambiguous dilemmas and help make them more aware of the consequences of their actions and decisions. I also love playing and watching football, watching films, and listening to good albums.
